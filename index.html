AutopilotSnap: scan the Windows Autopilot QR code

AutopilotSnap is a free browser tool for technicians who prepare Windows devices. Scan the QR code that Windows shows during setup (OOBE), check the detected device and send a ready-made CSV (Manufacturer, Model, Serial number) to your IT team, who upload it to Intune as a corporate device identifier.

No sign-in, no Intune or Entra access, no backend, no analytics and no cookies. The QR code is decoded and the CSV is created in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Open the Autopilot menu on the first OOBE screen by pressing the Windows key five times, choose "Scan QR code", then point your phone camera at it or upload a photo of the code. Several devices can go into one CSV.

Independent community project by msnugget, not affiliated with Microsoft. Based on the idea of Simon's open-source AutopilotQR (MIT). Requires JavaScript.
